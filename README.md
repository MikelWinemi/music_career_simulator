# Music Career Simulator

A comprehensive Flutter-based music career simulation game where players build their musical empire from aspiring artist to global superstar.

## Features

### Core Gameplay
- **Character Creation**: Create your musical persona with custom avatars and styles
- **Skill Development**: Level up songwriting, vocals, charisma, and other musical skills
- **Career Progression**: Rise from small gigs to sold-out stadium concerts

### Music Creation & Management
- **Song Writing**: Create original songs across multiple genres including Pop, Rock, Hip-Hop, R&B, Electronic, Jazz, and more
- **Album Production**: Organize songs into albums for bigger releases and enhanced popularity
- **Cover Images**: Add custom cover art to your singles and albums using photos from your gallery or camera
- **Song Popularity System**: Watch your songs grow in popularity over time with realistic streaming mechanics
- **Chart Performance**: Track your songs on the charts and achieve hit status

### Social & Collaboration Features
- **Group/Band Management**: Form and manage musical groups with other artists
- **Collaborative Albums**: Work with producers and other artists on joint projects
- **Industry Networking**: Build relationships with key industry figures
- **Social Media Promotion**: Promote your music across various platforms

### Business & Lifestyle
- **Financial Management**: Earn money from songs, concerts, and endorsements
- **Record Deals**: Sign with record labels for career boosts
- **Award System**: Win prestigious music awards based on your achievements
- **Lifestyle Choices**: Manage fame, stress, and personal relationships

### Technical Features
- **Persistent Game State**: Automatic save/load system maintains your progress
- **Image Management**: Built-in photo picker for custom album and single covers
- **Backwards Compatibility**: Seamless updates preserve existing save data
- **Real-time Statistics**: Track streams, revenue, and popularity in real-time

## Installation

1. Ensure you have Flutter installed on your system
2. Clone this repository
3. Run `flutter pub get` to install dependencies
4. Run `flutter run` to start the game

## Dependencies

- **image_picker**: For selecting cover images from gallery or camera
- **path_provider**: For managing image file storage
- **flutter**: Core Flutter framework

## Game Mechanics

### Popularity System
- Songs start with base popularity based on your fame level and song quality
- Popularity grows organically over time through streaming
- High-quality songs have better growth potential
- Promotion activities can boost popularity spikes

### Group Dynamics
- Form bands with up to 4 members
- Shared revenue and popularity benefits
- Collaborative decision making for group activities

### Genre System
- 16+ different music genres including the unique "Rage" genre
- Genre affects fan demographics and market appeal
- Cross-genre experimentation unlocks new audiences

### Award Categories
- Best Song, Best Album, Best New Artist
- Genre-specific awards (Best Rock Song, Best Pop Album, etc.)
- Lifetime Achievement awards for career milestones
